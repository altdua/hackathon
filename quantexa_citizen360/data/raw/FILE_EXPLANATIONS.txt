Quantexa mock data — what each file is
=====================================
Generated 10 Sep 2026. All people and identifiers are fictitious.
Phones use the Ofcom drama range 07700 900xxx.

These files pretend to be extracts from five disconnected systems.
They are deliberately inconsistent (names, dates, postcodes, address order)
so an entity-resolution step has something to do.


1. mcc_housing_tenancies_extract.csv
------------------------------------
Pretend source: Manchester City Council housing management system
                (styled as an Orchard Housing nightly extract).

What it contains:
  Current and former council tenancies. One row per tenancy.
  Fields include tenancy/property refs, name in SURNAME + FORENAME
  (often an initial), DOB as dd/mm/yyyy, address, postcode with or
  without a space, rent, weeks in arrears, last housing-officer visit
  and outcome, open ASB case refs, contact telephone.

Why it is messy:
  Maggie Hale is stored as HALE / M, DOB 12/03/1959, postcode M46LN
  (no space). ASB is logged against the PROPERTY, not as a finding
  that she is the perpetrator. Her son Daniel appears as a FORMER
  tenancy (evicted), same mobile number.


2. gp_emis_extract_mft.json
---------------------------
Pretend source: GP clinical system export
                (EMIS Web, packaged as an FHIR-like Bundle).

What it contains:
  Patient demographics, conditions and appointments in one JSON
  document (a single object — this is .json, not .jsonl).
  NHS number lives only in this file.

Why it is messy:
  Maggie appears as "Maggie Hale" with an old name "Margaret Hale".
  DOB is ISO 1959-03-12. Postcode is M4 6LN (with space).
  Household status = lives alone. Active depression code.
  Three appointments with status "noshow" (DNA) on 14 Jul, 4 Aug
  and 1 Sep 2026.

  Also includes two different John Smiths so you can test a refused
  merge (same name, adjacent Hulme addresses, different DOBs).


3. adult_social_care_mosaic_extract.csv
   adult_social_care_mosaic_extract.xlsx
---------------------------------------
Pretend source: Adult Social Care case system
                (Mosaic export). CSV is the raw table.
                XLSX is the same data with a typical extract banner,
                frozen header and filter row.

What it contains:
  Case ID, internal PersonRef (not an NHS number), display name,
  a concatenated AgeSex field, loosely typed DOB text, address as
  a single string, contact date, pathway, case status, allocated
  team/worker, RAG, notes.

Why it is messy:
  Maggie is "Hale / female", AgeSex = "67y female", DOB = "Mar 1959".
  Address order is "Ancoats, 18 Bengal St, Manchester".
  Case ASC/26/10994 is Open - low level.
  AllocatedWorker is blank (duty queue, no named worker).
  No Care Act assessment.


4. foodbank_visits_ancoats.jsonl
--------------------------------
Pretend source: Ancoats Community Pantry tap-log
                (local Trussell-style visit log dumped to JSON Lines).

Format:
  .jsonl = JSON Lines. One JSON object per line. Not a wrapping
  array. Read with json.loads() on each line, not json.load()
  on the whole file.

What it contains:
  Visit id, timestamp, first name only, postcode, household size,
  parcels issued, referral organisation, free-text notes.

Why it is messy:
  No surname, no DOB, no NHS number.
  Maggie appears as "Maggie" and once as "Mags".
  Postcode varies: "M4 6LN", "M46LN", "m4 6ln".
  Four visits in six weeks: 28 Jul, 11 Aug, 25 Aug, 8 Sep 2026.


5. revenues_and_energy_debt_flags.csv
-------------------------------------
Pretend source: Council revenues nightly extract joined to a
                district-heat / energy debt flag.

What it contains:
  Account reference, account type (council tax or heat recharge),
  account name as initials, a single address string, postcode,
  balance, recovery stage, last payment, energy debt band.
  No DOB. No NI number. UPRN is present and is a usable join key
  when it exists.

Why it is messy:
  Maggie is "HALE M" on CTAX-M4-882341 (CT arrears £614.22) and
  again on HEAT-ON-88421 (heat debt). Postcode mixed M4 6LN / M46LN.
  Daniel Hale has a separate Gorton CT account still in recovery.


6. GROUND_TRUTH.md
------------------
Not a source system. Team-only cheat sheet.
Says who should merge, who must not merge, and the household
edge (Daniel Hale shares a mobile with Maggie but is a different
person). Do not put this file on a demo slide.
